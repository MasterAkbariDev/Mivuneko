# 2D companions

Mivuneko includes Luna, an unofficial Miku fan-art companion, Kiko the fox, and Stella the star companion. Luna retains her original two expression sheets. The three newer AI-generated sheets each provide six drawn poses; additional expressions reuse those poses with distinct movement and effects. Speaking switches the mouth pose in response to playback volume rather than phonemes.

In Settings, choose a character, companion mode, expression, size, captions, motion and effects. The pet and Conversations window share these preferences. Old prototype and VRM selections fall back to Luna. Previously imported user VRM data is not deleted by this migration.

See [character sources](CHARACTER_SOURCES.md) and [asset provenance](../licenses/README.md) before distributing character art.
